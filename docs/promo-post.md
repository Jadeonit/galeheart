## Posting tips

- Attach the poster, not a wall of text. People scroll.
- Post in `#self-promotion`, `#advertising`, `#server-ads` — read the channel rules first.
- One post per server, no bump spam. Getting muted kills the reach.
- Best window: 17:00–21:00 UTC, when EU is awake and US East is starting.
- Reply to questions fast for the first hour — activity pushes the message up.
- Do not promise what the server does not have (no "custom classes", no "24/7 staff").
